# Speaker Notes

Speaker notes are spoken teaching, not production metadata or a written report. The instructor should be able to read them continuously while students understand the slide's meaning and its place in the argument.

## Recommended movement within one slide

Use only the functions the slide needs, in a natural order:

1. connect to the preceding idea or activate experience
2. state the question or conclusion
3. explain the concept, case, or evidence in learner language
4. give the range, condition, or limitation that changes interpretation
5. state the judgment learners should retain
6. explain why the next idea is needed

Do not turn this into six labeled sections. Two or three coherent paragraphs are often enough, but clarity takes priority over a fixed paragraph count.

## Write spoken Korean

- Prefer short, complete sentences with one main thought.
- Put the conclusion before dates, sample sizes, method names, or tool names unless chronology itself is the lesson.
- Use a concrete subject and verb. Replace noun-heavy report prose with something the instructor would say aloud.
- Remove translated metaphors that do not sound natural in Korean.
- Vary transitions; do not begin every slide with `이제`, `그러면`, or `다음으로`.

Common repairs:

| Awkward or production-like | Natural lecture wording |
|---|---|
| 오늘은 세 가지를 가져가면 됩니다 | 오늘은 세 가지를 말씀드리겠습니다 |
| 학생에게 필요한 이동 | 학생이 무엇을 바꿔야 하는지 |
| 차이를 건넌 계기 | 새로운 전환점이 된 계기 |
| 동사의 강도를 읽습니다 | 의무의 차이를 살펴봅니다 |
| 이 네 칸은 1:1 배치표가 아닙니다 | 네 질문은 일을 서로 다른 관점에서 살펴보게 합니다 |
| 현재 답: 파일럿 | 지금은 제한된 범위에서 시험하고 근거를 모을 단계입니다 |

These are examples, not a replacement dictionary. Rewrite the underlying idea.

## Explain before naming

At first use, introduce unfamiliar language in this order:

1. plain-language role or problem
2. formal name or acronym
3. a concrete consequence or example

Examples:

> 회사의 재무·구매·재고 같은 핵심 업무를 하나로 관리하는 시스템을 전사적 자원관리, 즉 ERP라고 합니다.

> 영국에는 시험과 자격을 감독하는 오프퀄(Ofqual)이라는 기관이 있습니다.

For people, explain why the person matters before recounting details. For difficult names, add a Korean pronunciation at first use when it helps the instructor. Do not add phonetic markup to every English word.

Never assume a term is familiar merely because it appears on the slide. If the explanation would overwhelm the slide, add a bridge slide or simplify the content.

## Describe meaning, not coordinates

Avoid narrating layout:

- `왼쪽 카드에는`
- `위쪽 원부터`
- `가로축을 보시면`
- `하단 화살표는`
- `화면처럼 세 묶음`

Explain the conceptual relation instead:

- `기업의 도입 깊이는 시험, 반복 사용, 전사 운영이라는 세 상태로 구분할 수 있습니다.`
- `논문 공개일이 최근일수록 오른쪽에 놓입니다. 점의 높이는 학습에 사용한 계산량을 뜻합니다.`
- `통제는 마지막 단계가 아니라 규칙·AI·사람이 일하는 전 과정을 감쌉니다.`

Mention reading order only when it carries causal, temporal, or procedural meaning.

## Teach how to read evidence

Keep these items close to a number:

- what was measured
- the denominator or population
- the date or period
- observed result, estimate, forecast, self-report, or vendor report
- what inference the number supports

Turn defensive disclaimers into explanatory limits:

> 공개 자료에서 확인된 대형 시스템을 센 값이므로 시장점유율보다는 계산 자원이 어디에 집중되는지 보여 줍니다.

> 2025년 수치는 현재 추정이고 2030년 수치는 중심 전망입니다. 숫자 하나보다 몇 년 사이 거의 두 배로 커지는 방향을 읽어야 합니다.

> 이 결과는 회사와 공급자가 발표한 성과입니다. 독립적인 인과평가라기보다 실제 운영 흐름이 어떻게 바뀌었는지 보여 주는 사례로 보겠습니다.

Do not bury the lecture in caveats, but never remove a condition that would change the learner's conclusion.

## Law and policy

Learners usually need the decision logic before article numbers. Explain:

1. who is covered
2. what activity or system is covered
3. what is required or prohibited
4. when it applies
5. the conditions and exceptions that change the answer
6. what a learner or organization should check

Preserve legal precision in the source record. On the slide and in notes, use plain language without turning a pending rule into current law or a statutory maximum into an actual fine.

## Cases and frameworks

- State the operational problem before the company name or technology.
- State the conclusion before implementation detail.
- Identify company-reported results as such.
- Do not present a classroom example as observed company performance.
- When a teaching framework is not an official standard, normally explain how to use it rather than narrating its production history. Mention its unofficial status only when confusion would affect a real decision.

## First and last slides

The first note should provide a short greeting, the governing question, and—when relevant—the course-level arc. It should not read the title block.

The last note should recover the judgment tool and apply it. When previewing another session, explain the unresolved question that leads there.

## Note hygiene

Follow the project's explicit policy. For a read-aloud script, the useful default is:

- keep only spoken prose in embedded notes
- keep time codes, URLs, DOI strings, access dates, production labels, and citation lists in a separate source record
- avoid `도입:`, `설명:`, `전환:`, and stage directions
- do not compress prose to a TTS allocation unless the user requests timing optimization

Keep embedded notes, the canonical manuscript, and the authoring source exactly synchronized when all three exist.

## Full-deck read-through

Read the deck from beginning to end and ask:

- Does any term appear before it is explained?
- Does a major change in time, scale, lens, or evidence occur without a reason?
- Does a note refer to a chart, map, image, or card that is not on screen?
- Does any sentence sound like a reviewer response, production memo, or machine translation?
- Do repeated transition phrases make the lecture feel mechanical?
- Are the opening question and closing judgment connected?
